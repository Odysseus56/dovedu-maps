2. What the app does

  • It computes your phone's position on the phone itself, from satellite signals, motion sensors, Android's network location, mobile cells and Wi-Fi, and, if you connect one, an OBD adapter in your car.
  • It shows that position on a map.
  • It tells you when it cannot stand behind a position, instead of guessing.
  • It finds addresses and draws a route line to your destination.
  • While positioning is on (from when you open the app until you tap Stop, or until it shuts down by itself after the car has stood for a while), it records your trips on your phone, as the Privacy Policy explains.

It does not give turn-by-turn or voice guidance.

Please read this section.

  • Maps, addresses and routes can be wrong or out of date. Roads close, and maps have errors. A route may lead to closed, restricted or unsafe places. Road signs, the police and official instructions always come before the app.
  • You are the driver, and you stay responsible for how you drive, where you go and for following traffic rules. Watch the road, not the phone. Set your destination before you drive, or let a passenger do it.
  • Do not use the app for emergencies or safety-critical tasks: calling or guiding emergency services, rescue, aviation, boating, or anything where a wrong position could hurt someone. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Ukraine and the European Union).
